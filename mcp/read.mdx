## search_contacts

Finds contacts by free text (name, email or title) and filters.

| Parameter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `query` | Optional text matched against email, first name, last name and title |
| `status` | Optional [contact status](/concepts/data-model#value-sets) |
| `company_id` | Optional company |
| `tag` | Optional tag, for example a list name |
| `limit` | Optional, default 25, max 200 |
| `offset` | Optional, for paging |

Results are ordered by most recent activity and include the company.

## get_contact

Takes `contact`: a contact ID or email. Returns one contact with company, deals, campaign enrollments and the 25 most recent activities.

## search_companies

Finds companies by name, domain or industry, with contact and deal counts.

## account_timeline

Everything that happened with one account across every tool: the company, its contacts, its deals and up to 100 activities, newest first. Takes `company`: a company ID or a domain such as `acme.com`.

## get_pipeline

Deals grouped by stage with the count and total value per stage, plus open count, open value and closed-won value. Pass `include_closed: true` to include the won and lost columns (default `false`).

## list_campaigns

Campaigns from every connected sequencer. Optional filters: `provider`, `status`.

## campaign_performance

Totals and per-campaign results: leads, sent, opened, replied, positive, bounced, plus open, reply, positive and bounce rates as percentages of sent. Optional `provider` filter.

## replied_without_deal

Contacts who replied by email or LinkedIn within `days` (default 30) and have no open deal. Each result includes which tool the reply came from, when, and whether the contact already exists in a CRM. This is the usual starting point for [sync_to_crm](/mcp/workflows#sync_to_crm).
