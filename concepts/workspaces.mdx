A workspace is one client company. Each workspace is an organization in Morton Street's sign-in system, and every record (contacts, deals, connections, audit entries) belongs to exactly one workspace.

## Members

Invite people under **Settings → Members**. Admins can change permissions and connect or disconnect tools.

## Which workspace the MCP acts in

An AI client signs in as you, not as a workspace. If you belong to one workspace, every call uses it. If you belong to several:

- `whoami` shows the active workspace and the others you can use.
- `switch_workspace` changes the default for your MCP sessions. The choice is saved, so your next session starts in the same workspace.

## Sponsor and portfolio view

A workspace can be marked as a **sponsor** (for example, a PE firm or an agency) and own other workspaces as its portfolio. Members of the sponsor workspace see a **Portfolio** page that rolls up, per portfolio company:

- contacts and companies
- open deals and pipeline value
- closed-won value
- active campaigns
- replies in the last 30 days
- connected tools

Sponsors can read their portfolio companies' data. Writes stay limited to the workspace you are signed in to, so a sponsor cannot change a portfolio company's CRM or campaigns from the sponsor workspace.
