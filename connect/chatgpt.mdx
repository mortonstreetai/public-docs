ChatGPT connects to remote MCP servers as apps and connectors. The same server Claude and Grok Bot use works for ChatGPT.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable developer mode if required">
    Depending on your plan, custom MCP connectors may need developer mode, which a workspace admin turns on under **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Under **Settings → Apps & Connectors**, create a new connector:

    - **Name:** Morton Street
    - **MCP server URL:** `https://api.mortonstreet.ai/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and use it">
    Sign in with your Morton Street account when prompted, then enable the connector in a chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>ChatGPT's menu names change often. If a label above does not match, look for "connectors" or "apps" in settings; the URL and OAuth choice are what matter. All connection details, plus troubleshooting, are on [Custom connector](/connect/custom-connector).</Info>
