---
title: "Claude"
description: "Add Morton Street as a custom connector in Claude."
---

Morton Street is a remote MCP server with OAuth sign-in, so Claude can connect to it directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector**, and enter:

    - **Name:** Morton Street
    - **URL:** `https://api.mortonstreet.ai/mcp`

    On Team and Enterprise plans an owner may need to add it for the organization first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect**. A Morton Street sign-in window opens. Sign in with your portal account and approve access. Claude registers itself automatically; there is no client ID to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    Enable the connector from the tools menu in a conversation and ask something like "What does our pipeline look like by stage?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you belong to more than one workspace, ask Claude to call `whoami` to see which workspace it is acting in, and `switch_workspace` to change it. See [Workspace tools](/mcp/workspace).
</Note>
