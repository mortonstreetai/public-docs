## Connect

Paste your Smartlead API key from **Settings → Your API key**.

## What syncs

Up to 100 campaigns with status and analytics: sent, unique opens, replies, interested leads (counted as positive) and bounces.

## Writes

`push_leads_to_sequence` uploads leads in batches of 100 with name, company, website, phone, LinkedIn URL and job title. Smartlead's global block list and unsubscribe list are always respected.
