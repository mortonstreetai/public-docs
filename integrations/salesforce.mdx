## Connect

Click **Connect with Salesforce** and sign in. Morton Street requests `api`, `refresh_token` and `offline_access`. Your org must allow API access (Enterprise edition and above, or Professional with API enabled).

<Note>Salesforce requires a Connected App. Listing on AppExchange also needs a security review, so a per-client Connected App may be used during onboarding.</Note>

## What syncs

- Contacts with their account name and website
- Opportunities with stage, amount, close date and account

## Writes

- Contacts are matched on email, then updated or created.
- Opportunities are created with a close date 30 days out, and stage changes are written back.
- Notes are logged as completed Tasks on the contact.

## Stage mapping
