title: "HeyReach"
description: "LinkedIn outreach campaigns across your sender accounts."
---

## Connect

Paste your HeyReach API key from **Integrations → Public API**.

## What syncs

Campaigns with status and progress: total leads, contacted and replied.

## Writes

`push_leads_to_sequence` adds leads by **LinkedIn profile URL**, in batches of 100, with name, company, title and email. Contacts without a LinkedIn URL are skipped, and the preview tells you how many.
