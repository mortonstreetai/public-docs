---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "From a new workspace to your first cross-tool question in Claude."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and create your workspace">
    Go to [app.mortonstreet.ai](https://app.mortonstreet.ai) and sign in. Create an organization for your company; that organization is your workspace. Invite teammates from **Settings → Members**.

    Under **Settings → Workspace**, add your company domain so your logo shows up across the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Open **Integrations**. HubSpot, Salesforce and Shopify connect with a sign-in button. Instantly, Smartlead and HeyReach take an API key. Odoo takes your instance URL, database, login and an API key.

    Morton Street checks every credential with the vendor before saving it, then runs a first sync. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for what each one needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the shared data">
    The **Overview**, **Contacts**, **Pipeline** and **Campaigns** pages now show records from every connected tool. No tools yet? Use **Load sample data** on the Overview page to explore with a demo dataset tagged `sample`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI client">
    Add `https://api.mortonstreet.ai/mcp` to [Claude](/connect/claude), [ChatGPT](/connect/chatgpt), [Claude Code](/connect/claude-code) or [Cursor](/connect/cursor). You sign in with the same Morton Street account; no API keys are pasted into the AI client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a cross-tool question">
    Try:

    > Who replied to a campaign in the last 30 days but has no open deal? Create them in HubSpot with a deal at "meeting".

    The assistant calls `replied_without_deal`, then `sync_to_crm`. Because that is a bulk CRM write, you get a preview first and confirm it with `confirm_action`, or approve it in the portal under **Approvals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
